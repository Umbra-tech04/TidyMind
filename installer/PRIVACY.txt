TidyMind - Privacy

Everything stays on this computer.

- No account and no sign-in.
- No network: TidyMind does not connect to the internet and sends nothing
  anywhere.
- No tracking, no ads, no usage statistics.

Where your data is stored
Your memories, projects, collections, notes, to-dos, reminders, pictures and
attached files are all kept in one folder:

  %LOCALAPPDATA%\TidyMind
  (usually C:\Users\<your name>\AppData\Local\TidyMind)

Settings > Open data folder shows it.

Reminders
Each reminder adds a task to Windows Task Scheduler named
TidyMind_Reminder_..., which starts TidyMind at the reminder's time.
When it's due, Windows shows its title and message as a notification and
keeps it in the notification center until you clear it.
Uninstalling TidyMind removes these tasks.

Backing up
Settings > Back up all data saves everything into one ZIP file, wherever you
choose. Settings > Restore from backup puts it back.

Deleting your data
When you uninstall TidyMind, it asks whether to delete this folder too. The
answer is No unless you choose Yes. You can also delete the folder yourself at
any time while TidyMind is closed.
