TidyMind 1.1.0

- TidyMind can now update itself. When a new version is out, it asks once at start; "Update now" downloads it, closes TidyMind, installs it and starts it again. Your data stays as it is.
- Settings has a new Updates section: the installed version, "Check now", and a switch to turn the automatic check off.
- Exports to PDF, Word and Excel were redesigned.
