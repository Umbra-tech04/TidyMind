- Vehicles get their own window: a large picture gallery, the key facts and upcoming deadlines on top, and tabs for an overview, specs (identity, engine, body, ownership, odometer), documents with files and reminders, and the service and fuel logs, each section edited in place; "Update odometer" keeps a history; a currency setting for prices.
- Vehicles also keep their wheel sets (tyres, rims, bolts, which set is mounted), fluids and filters, brakes, battery and bulbs, and a tree of parts (system > unit > part) with part numbers, specs, photos and fitting dates; one search box finds anything in the vehicle.
- The vehicle's service log: services, repairs, modifications, taxes and fees with the odometer, the cost and the parts fitted (each part keeps its history), and when the next one is due, by date (with a reminder) or by kilometres; a filter by kind, and the costs per kind and per year. Entries from the old service log carry over as they are.
- Item windows stay put when you switch pages, forms fit at any width, a garment's sizes are one table (pieces with - and +, edit and delete on the line), and in every task list the open tasks are listed above the done ones.
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y): deletes, ticks, renames, moves and saved edits can be taken back, with an Undo button right after a delete; deleted items, projects and memories come back with their pictures, files and reminders.
- Every memory has its own notebook for notes about the whole memory (the Notebook button on its page): the same pages and formatting as a project's notes, included in exports, backups and a collection's transfer, and found by search.
- Easier to find: a short tour of the main window, Tips & shortcuts under the ? button (the right-click menus, keys and gestures), a ⋯ button on cards and memory pages for their menus, Search in the sidebar, a Getting started list on Home, and a "What's new" note after an update.
- A new app icon, in the program, the taskbar, the Start menu and the installer.
