New
- A collection item can now hold several pictures, for example one car photographed from different angles: add them together, choose the cover, reorder or remove them, and click a small picture to see it larger.
- Quick note is now an ordinary window: it no longer stays on top of other programs, and closing it takes you back to exactly where you were in TidyMind.
