TidyMind 1.5.1

New
- Custom collections: in an empty collection, choose Custom when you add the first item, then build your own item page from blocks: fields, pictures, notes, checklists, documents with reminders, a log and quantities.
- You build the page on the page itself. Switch on Customize, add blocks with "+ Add", type a field's name to add it and pick its type beside it, and rename, move, resize or remove a block from its toolbar. Up to three fields can show on the cards.
- The dates in your custom items show in the Day Plan, and they go along in exports, transfer packages and backups.

Fixed
- Custom collections: the first page of a new custom collection is now a real item, so pictures, checklist lines and everything else can be added right away.

Note: your data is stored on your computer without encryption, so avoid storing sensitive data, or use BitLocker.
