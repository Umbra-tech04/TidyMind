TidyMind 1.3.0

New
- Several pictures per collection item: add up to 20 at once, for example one car photographed from different angles. The first picture is the cover, the card shows how many there are, and in the item you can browse them, reorder them or remove one.

Improved
- Quick note is now an ordinary window: it no longer stays on top of other programs, and Esc takes you back to exactly where you were in TidyMind.
