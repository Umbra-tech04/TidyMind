TidyMind 1.2.0

New
- Delete several things at once: hold Ctrl (or Shift) and click to select memories, collections, projects, items, tasks, files, Day Plan blocks, to-dos, reminders or notebook pages, then delete them together.
- Move things to another PC: right-click a project, a collection or a single item and choose "Export for transfer…" to save it as a .tidymind file. On the other PC, open it with Settings > Backup & transfer > Import.
- Settings has a new look: categories on the left, each with its own page.
- Adaptive card tint: over a background picture, the header becomes quiet frosted glass, so text stays readable and your cards stay in focus. You can switch it off in Settings > Appearance.

Improved
- TidyMind now looks for updates every time it starts. When a new version is out, an "Update available" button in the sidebar lets you install it whenever it suits you.
- Background pictures look clearer, and a slider in Settings > Appearance sets how strongly they show.
- Better-looking PDF, Word and Excel exports.
