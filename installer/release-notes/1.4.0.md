TidyMind 1.4.0

New
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y). Deleted items, projects, memories, tasks and files, ticks, renames, moves and saved edits can all be taken back, and an Undo button appears right after a delete. Deleted things come back with their pictures, files and reminders.
- Every memory now has its own notebook for notes about the whole memory (the Notebook button on its page). It has the same pages and formatting as a project's notes, and it is included in exports, backups and a collection's transfer, and found by search.
- Vehicles have their own window, with a large picture gallery, the key facts and upcoming deadlines on top, and tabs for an overview, specs, documents, wheels and tyres, fluids and parts, and the service log. Each section is edited right where it is, and one search box finds anything in the vehicle.
- Documents (insurance, inspection and more) keep their files and can remind you a number of days before they expire.
- Wheels and tyres: keep your wheel sets (tyres, rims, bolts) and mark which set is on the car.
- Fluids and filters, brakes, battery and bulbs, and a tree of parts (system, unit, part) with part numbers, specs, photos and fitting dates.
- Service log and costs: services, repairs, modifications and taxes with the odometer, the cost and the parts fitted. Each part keeps its history. Set when the next one is due, by date (with a reminder) or by kilometres, filter by kind, and see the costs per kind and per year. Entries from the old service log carry over as they are.
- "Update odometer" keeps a history, and there is a currency setting for prices.
- A new app icon.

Improved
- Easier to find your way around: a short tour of the main window, Tips & shortcuts under the ? button (the right-click menus, keys and gestures), a "What's new" note after an update, and a Getting started list on Home.
- Cards and memory pages have a visible menu button, and Search has its own entry in the sidebar.
- In every task list the open tasks are listed above the done ones.
- A garment's sizes are now one table, with - and + for the pieces and edit and delete on each line.

Fixed
- Switching pages in an item window no longer moves the pictures and the title.
- Forms in item windows fit at any window width, with nothing cut off.
